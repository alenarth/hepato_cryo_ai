HepatoCryoAI

Predictive tool for HepG2 cell viability after cryopreservation

Chronic liver diseases represent a major public health burden, with cirrhosis being the leading cause of hospitalizations and mortality in Brazil. Liver transplantation remains the only effective therapy, but the shortage of available organs versus rising demand highlights the need for alternatives such as human hepatocyte cell therapy. The main limitation of this approach is the loss of cell viability and functionality after thawing, underscoring the importance of optimized cryopreservation protocols.

Objective

HepatoCryoAI supports the optimization of hepatocyte cryopreservation protocols through machine learning algorithms that predict post-thaw viability of HepG2 cells from the DMSO and trehalose concentrations of the formulation. The tool employs two distinct algorithms trained on the same dataset — a Random Forest Regressor and a neural network (trained in PyTorch, served with NumPy) — and acts as a calculator for protocol simulation.

Perspectives

HepatoCryoAI is a screening tool for cryoprotectant concentrations: a combination of DMSO and trehalose can be evaluated with the models before it is tested at the bench, so that wet-lab experiments in the optimization of hepatocyte cryopreservation protocols can be prioritized.

Disclaimer

HepatoCryoAI is provided solely as a complementary research tool. Results from this simulation should not replace rigorous in vitro and in vivo laboratory validation protocols. Responsibility for decisions based on this tool lies entirely with the researcher and their institution.
